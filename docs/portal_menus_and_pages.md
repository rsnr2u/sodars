# SODARS Complete Portal Menus & Pages Documentation
## Streamline Outdoor Advertising Reach Solutions

This document defines the complete menus, sections, sub-pages, filters, and settings for all portals, mobile applications, and common systems in the **SODARS** (Streamline Outdoor Advertising Reach Solutions) platform.

# 1. WEBSITE PORTAL
### Domain: `www.sodars.com`
### Purpose:
Public B2B marketplace platform for advertisers, agencies, brands, and consumers to discover, search, compare, and request bookings of outdoor media and hoarding inventory.

### 🌐 Main Header Menus
* **Home**: Main portal lander.
* **Marketplace**: B2B search catalog grids.
* **Digital Screens**: High-visibility LED listings.
* **Transit Media**: Mobile transit billboard listings.
* **Cities**: Top operational nodes directories.
* **Providers**: Curation of partner media vendors.
* **Campaigns**: Dynamic multi-city campaigns creator.
* **About Us**: Platforms core vision.
* **Contact Us**: Outbound telecalling support.
* **Login**: Entry gateway.

### 🏠 Homepage Sections
* **Hero Banner**: High-value search action.
* **Search Inventory**: Zipcode/City filter bar.
* **Featured Inventory**: Priority-ranked spaces showcase.
* **Top Cities**: Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi nodes.
* **Premium Providers**: Certified partners slider.
* **Digital Screens**: Digital displays slider.
* **Campaign Solutions**: Corporate campaigns solutions layouts.
* **How It Works**: 4-step consumer explanation.
* **Testimonials**: Buyer validation sliders.
* **Blogs**: Dynamic SEO content.
* **Contact CTA**: Lead generation forms.

### 🛒 Marketplace Pages
* **Inventory Listing**: Search database grids.
* **Inventory Details**: specs, galleries, rates, calendars, mapping.
* **Nearby Inventory**: Geofenced proximity listings.
* **Map View**: Split-screen Google Maps SDK coordinates pins.
* **Featured Listings**: Premium billing listings.
* **Provider Listings**: Complete verified vendors directory.
* **Campaign Inquiry**: Multi-city campaigns briefs submissions.
* **Search Results**: Autocomplete matched outputs.

### 🔍 Search Filters
* **Geospatial Taxonomy**: Country, State, District, City, Area, Road, Landmark.
* **Inventory Specs**: Media Type (Hoarding, LED, etc.), Monthly Price, Lighting Class, Dimensions, Calendar Availability, Traffic Score.

### 👤 Customer Pages
* **Authentication**: Register, Login, Forgot Password.
* **Dashboard**: User dashboard workspace.
* **My Inquiries**: Logs of submitted inquiries.
* **My Campaigns**: Campaign brief drafts.
* **Invoices**: Tax receipt invoice downloads.
* **Profile**: Personal contact details.

### 📄 Informational Pages
* **Corporate Details**: About, Support.
* **Legal Declarations**: Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy.
* **Content Nodes**: Blogs, Careers.
